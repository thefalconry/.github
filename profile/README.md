# 🦅 The Falconry

**Autonomy with a human at the other end of the glove.**

Falconry is an old partnership. The bird flies free and hunts on its own judgement, and it comes back to the glove. That's how we think long-lived AI agents should work: real independence, with a person who stays accountable for what the agent does.

## Talon

[**Talon**](https://github.com/thefalconry/talon) is an open-source, self-hosted harness for long-lived AI agents. It gives an agent persistent memory, tools, scheduled work, a mesh of companion devices, and chat frontends (Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Teams, terminal, native apps), and it runs on hardware you control.

- 🧠 persistent memory and a knowledge palace
- 🛠 MCP tools and plugins, sub-agents, triggers and cron
- 📱 companion apps and headless nodes for your own devices
- 🔐 encrypted backups, scoped access, and nothing leaves your box unless you say so

## What we believe

An AI agent that acts in the world should be **checkable**: a name on its work, a record for anyone it acts on, and the right to refuse an unlawful instruction on the record.
